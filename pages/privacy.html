NEXUS // PRIVACY

YOUR DATA. YOUR CONTROL.

This privacy policy explains, in plain language, how the current NEXUS website handles information provided through its front-end features.

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 15, 2026
01 / INFORMATION WE COLLECT

What the current site handles.

The main site includes forms and interactive tools that can process information you enter in your browser. For example, the membership form can collect your name, email address and selected objective while the AI Coach can use the options you select to generate a training protocol.

02 / LOCAL STORAGE

Saved AI Coach plans.

The current AI Coach can save a generated plan in your browser's local storage so that it can be restored on the AI Coach page. This is stored locally in your browser and is not, by itself, a server-side database.

03 / CONTACT FORMS

What happens when you submit.

The current front-end contact form is a demonstration form and does not transmit a message to a backend. The membership form currently displays an in-browser confirmation and does not connect to a server unless a backend is added later.

04 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External resources.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When the browser requests those font resources, the request is made to Google's servers. Apart from resources explicitly added to the site later, you should update this policy if analytics, advertising, payment, CRM or other third-party services are connected.

05 / SECURITY & CHANGES

Keep this policy current.

This page describes the current front-end implementation. If you later connect a backend, analytics platform, email provider, payment processor, authentication system or advertising service, update this policy before collecting or sharing that information.

06 / CONTACT

Questions about privacy?

Use the contact page to send a privacy-related question after a backend or contact service has been connected. Replace this section with your real business contact details before publishing the site commercially.
